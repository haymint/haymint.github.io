# Images

hero.jpg is an AI-generated lifestyle illustration, labeled on the homepage. It does not depict a specific recommended product or demonstrate product use.

All product photos, Reel thumbnails and optional category photos must be your own. Do not download seller images, copy reference-screenshot photos, repost creator videos or use the Shopee logo.

Product paths: assets/img/products/your-photo.jpg
Video paths: assets/img/videos/your-thumbnail.jpg
Optional category paths: assets/img/categories/living.jpg (set taxonomy entry image property).

No product, video or category photographs are bundled. Missing optional category photos use icons. No fake product imagery is shown.
